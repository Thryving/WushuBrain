知其所以然

好的教练知其然——知道怎么练有效。
这三天，让你知其所以然：每一个马步、每一次眼随手走、每一套左右对称的动作，在孩子的大脑里究竟发生了什么。当你能把它讲清楚，家长的怀疑会变成信任，你的课会变成孩子成长里不可替代的一部分。

Good coaches know what works. These three days give you the why: what actually happens in a child's brain during every horse stance, every "eyes follow the hand", every left-right mirrored sequence. When you can explain it clearly, parents' doubt turns into trust — and your class becomes an irreplaceable part of a child's growth.

课程内容What the course covers

强健体魄 · 激活大脑 · 全面提升Strong body · Active brain · All-round growth

3天师资培训，全程中文授课，由胡仁杰讲师主讲。课程把武术桩功、身法与呼吸导引，融合进前庭、视动、原始反射与学习障碍干预体系——每个主题都有原理讲解，也有武术实操。

A 3-day teacher training, taught in Mandarin by Hu Renjie. Wushu standing practice, body work and breath guidance are woven into vestibular, visual-motor, primitive-reflex and learning-difficulty intervention — every topic pairs the principles with hands-on wushu practice.

武术基本功与协调训练

Wushu fundamentals & coordination

桩功与无极桩、沉肩立腰体态、对称与不对称动作、移步转体——用武术基本功重塑本体感觉、平衡与身体协调。

Standing practice and wuji post, upright posture, symmetrical and asymmetrical movement, stepping and turning — using wushu fundamentals to rebuild proprioception, balance and coordination.

脑功能与神经发育

Brain function & neurodevelopment

8种原始反射精讲、前庭功能测试体系、武术定式眼随训练——看懂孩子坐不住、易分心、站不稳背后的原因。

Eight primitive reflexes in depth, a vestibular function testing system and wushu "eyes follow" drills — understanding what sits behind restlessness, distractibility and poor balance.

记忆力与学习策略

Memory & learning strategies

视听融合记忆、武术口令跟随；阅读、书写、数学及非语言学习困难的解决策略。

Audio-visual memory and wushu call-and-follow drills; strategies for reading, writing, maths and non-verbal learning difficulties.

体脑结合的综合训练

Integrated body-brain training

武术呼吸导引调节情绪，支持自主神经调节；视动训练、光与声音刺激、多重任务及重力沙包综合练习。

Wushu breath guidance to regulate emotion and support autonomic nervous system regulation; visual-motor work, light and sound stimulation, multitasking and weighted-sandbag practice.

授课演练与干预方案设计

Teaching practicum & intervention design

分组演练一节完整的【脑优化 + 武术】干预课，并设计个性化落地方案，带回武馆、课室和家。

In groups, teach a complete "Brain Optimisation + Wushu" intervention lesson and design a personalised action plan to take back to your class, school or home.

实操非常多。每个主题都配有武术融合练习：例如原始反射配合静态站姿、前庭训练配合无极桩与眼随训练、学习困难配合移步与空间方位训练、情绪调节配合呼吸导引；第二天晚上有重力沙包练习，第三天更以分组演练一节完整的【脑优化 + 武术】干预课作结。请穿着方便活动的衣服和运动鞋。

A lot. 什么是“原始反射”？为什么教练和老师需要懂？What are primitive reflexes, and why should coaches and teachers care?

原始反射是婴儿与生俱来的自动反应（例如抓握反射），一般会在成长过程中被更成熟的动作控制取代。如果某些反射未能完全整合，可能表现为姿势不稳、坐不住、手眼协调差或书写吃力。第一天会精讲8种原始反射，并实操武术静态站姿如何帮助抑制未整合的反射。

Primitive reflexes are automatic movements babies are born with (the grasp reflex, for example), normally replaced by more mature control as a child develops. When some aren't fully integrated, they may show up as poor posture, restlessness, weak eye-hand coordination or effortful handwriting. Day 1 covers eight primitive reflexes in depth, with hands-on practice of how static wushu stances can help inhibit unintegrated reflexes.

课程会教我怎么评估孩子吗？Will I learn how to assess children?

你会学到前庭功能测试体系（前庭眼动、前庭脊髓、前庭小脑）及原始反射的观察方法，用来了解孩子的状况、设计训练内容。这些是教育与训练用途的观察工具，不是医学诊断；如发现明显困难，建议转介专业人士评估。

You'll learn a vestibular function testing system (vestibulo-ocular, vestibulo-spinal, vestibulo-cerebellar) and how to observe primitive reflexes, so you can understand a child and design training. These are observation tools for education and training, not medical diagnosis; where you see significant difficulties, refer the child for professional assessment.

课程有讲到ADHD、读写困难、数学困难吗？Does it cover ADHD, reading/writing and maths difficulties?

有。第二天涵盖阅读与书写障碍、数学困难、非语言学习困难的解决策略，以及左右脑整合障碍（ADHD）的干预与镜像疗法；第三天包括语言与书写困难训练。每个主题都配合相应的武术训练。这些是支持性训练策略，不取代医生或治疗师的专业治疗。

Yes. Day 2 covers reading and writing difficulties, maths and non-verbal learning difficulties, and bilateral integration difficulties (ADHD) with mirror-therapy techniques; Day 3 covers language and handwriting difficulties — each paired with wushu training. These are supportive training strategies and do not replace professional treatment.

孩子最终能得到什么改变？What changes can children gain?

课程围绕五个方向：提升专注力（减少分心）、增强记忆力（记得快、记得稳）、改善学习能力（逻辑思考、解决问题）、情绪管理（更冷静、更有耐心）以及通过武术训练提升协调、平衡、意志力与纪律性。每个孩子起点不同，进步需要持续练习；这三天让你知道该练什么、为什么练。

The course works towards five areas: better focus (fewer distractions), stronger memory, improved learning ability (logical thinking, problem-solving), emotional balance (calmer, more patient), and — through wushu training — coordination, balance, willpower and discipline. Every child starts from a different place and progress takes consistent practice; these three days show you what to train and why.

课程内容跟武术、太极有直接关系吗？Is the content directly about wushu and taichi?

Yes — throughout all three days.
